# Play Store App Description

## App Name
Mist POS: AI Billing & Inventory

## Short Description (Max 80 characters)
A powerful Point of Sale, inventory tracker & AI assistant for your business.

## Full Description
Welcome to **Mist POS**, your ultimate all-in-one smart Point of Sale and Inventory Management solution. Designed to simplify and scale your business, Mist POS combines robust daily operations with cutting-edge **AI technology** to give you unparalleled control over your sales, inventory, and analytics.

Whether you run a single retail store, a vehicle fleet, or manage a reseller network, Mist POS is equipped with everything you need.

### 🌟 Key Features

**🤖 SuperAdmin AI Assistant**
Interact directly with your business data! Use voice or text commands to ask our intelligent AI Assistant for real-time sales queries, statistical aggregations, and insights. Simply talk to the AI to instantly filter dates, search demographics, and generate reports. 

**📦 Smart Inventory & Barcode Scanning**
Easily track your stock levels in real-time. Use the built-in barcode scanner to instantly add, update, or checkout items.

**📊 Advanced Analytics & Dashboards**
Visualize your success with interactive charts and dynamic dashboards. Track daily, monthly, and yearly revenue and expenses. Dive deep into analytics, including a dedicated Country Acquisition Dashboard to understand your customer demographics.

**🖨️ Multipoint Bluetooth Printing**
Print professional receipts effortlessly. Mist POS supports concurrent multipoint Bluetooth printing, allowing you to seamlessly connect and manage multiple POS printers simultaneously without connection drops.

**🤝 Reseller & Merchant Management**
Manage your network with a dedicated Reseller Dashboard. Generate comprehensive PDF reports and Excel sheets for monthly earnings, merchant statistics, and financial tracking.

**👥 Visitor & Customer Management**
Keep track of visitor registrations with precise timezone handling, manage customer subscriptions, and handle recurring payments effortlessly.

**🔒 Secure, Fast & Reliable**
Enjoy peace of mind with lightning-fast offline-capable databases, secure syncing, and a sleek, premium, user-friendly interface.
